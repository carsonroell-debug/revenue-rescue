# Revenue Rescue — Directory Listing Copy

**Name:** Revenue Rescue

**Tagline:** Find and monitor the website problems that can break revenue paths.

## Short description

Revenue Rescue gives AI agents a specialized website commerce diagnostic
engine. It audits public sites for confirmed problems such as dead merchant
destinations, affiliate attribution loss, discontinued offers, soft-404
product redirects, and unhealthy redirect chains, then returns structured
evidence and recommended actions.

It can also monitor a site over time and surface meaningful changes such as a
destination changing, tracking disappearing, a new revenue risk appearing, or
an existing risk being resolved.

## Long description

Revenue Rescue is built for publishers, creators, commerce operators, agencies,
and businesses that depend on websites to generate revenue.

Instead of asking an AI agent to guess whether a link or offer is healthy,
Revenue Rescue performs the underlying technical checks and returns
machine-readable evidence.

Current capabilities include:

- prioritizing commercially relevant pages;
- checking outbound merchant and affiliate destinations;
- following redirect chains safely;
- detecting confirmed 404/410 and server failures;
- detecting product soft-404 behavior;
- detecting visible affiliate tracking loss;
- extracting Product/Offer structured data;
- detecting explicitly discontinued structured offers;
- ranking confirmed findings by commercial context;
- assigning stable finding IDs for follow-up explanations;
- creating persistent site monitors;
- detecting destination, status, tracking, finding, and offer changes over time.

Revenue Rescue follows a precision-first policy. Bot blocks, timeouts, and
uncertain fetches are treated as inconclusive rather than being presented as
confirmed revenue leaks.

## Data access

Revenue Rescue audits public HTTP(S) pages supplied by the user. It does not
require website admin credentials for public-site audits.

Production access may require an API/connector credential and is rate-limited
to protect the service and scanned sites.
