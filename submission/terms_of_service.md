1. THE SERVICE
Revenue Rescue provides automated audits of public websites for revenue
leaks (dead affiliate links, missing tracking parameters, soft-404
redirects). Reports are informational.

2. ACCEPTABLE USE
- Audit only websites you own or have permission to audit.
- Do not use the service to probe, overload, or attack third-party sites.

3. ACCURACY

the destination (HTTP 404/410, server errors, soft-404 redirects). Results
marked inconclusive (bot-blocked pages, timeouts, connection failures) are
never reported as leaks. Even so, the web changes constantly: treat every
report as a starting point for human review, not a guarantee.
